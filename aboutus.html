About Us

Loving Homes is a modern dog hotel in the Hong Kong, designed to ensure that every dog receives comfort, safety and care at all times. We offer individual climate-controlled rooms, grooming and spa, walks, playgrounds and round-the-clock veterinary support to make pets feel at home. Our mission is to provide a high level of care, create a warm and peaceful environment for dogs and give owners full confidence that their pet is in reliable and professional hands.
