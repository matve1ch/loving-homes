Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our services.

What do I need to bring for my dog?

Please bring your dog’s food, any medication, and optionally their favorite toy.

Do you provide daily updates?

Yes, we send photos and short messages every day during your dog’s stay.

Can I visit the facilities before booking?

Absolutely! You can schedule a tour any day of the week.

Do you accept all breeds?

Yes, we welcome dogs of all sizes, ages, and breeds.
